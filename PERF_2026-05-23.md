## Micro-measurements: §228 and §229

These were measured one-shot in their respective validation runs; no
strict A/B but the absolute numbers are diagnostic.

### §228 (parallel xSq‖x1Sq via Parallel.Invoke)

`C:\PiOutput_228test` 1 B-digit `SafeMpzSqrt` final-adj region:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Operand size | 51.9 M-limb x, 51.9 M-limb x1 |
| Parallel pair pre-alloc | 792 MB × 2 |
| `Parallel.Invoke` wall | **102.26 s** |
| adj-down / adj-up iters | 0 / 0 (Newton converged exactly) |
| Effective cores during pair | 6.35 (26.5 % of 24-core box) |

Pre-§228 (`§207` force-serial `_safeMulDop = 1`): each squaring runs
on a single core's recursive SafeMpzMul.  Two squarings strictly
sequential, internal recursion at DOP 1.

The 1 B parallel-pair wall (102 s) is roughly equal to one
serial-recursion squaring; pre-§228 would run both serially ≈ 200 s.
**Saving at 1 B: ~100 s.**  At 5 B (~260 M-limb squarings → 10-20 h
each serial) the parallel pair projects to ~10-20 h vs ~20-40 h
serial — **~10-20 h saved on the #72 Phase 4 critical path.**

## Caveats

- Pre-ParaPerf baseline 5 B numbers (the 80 h figure in #72) are
  *projections* — the actual pre-ParaPerf 5 B has not been re-run
  since the in-flight 5B-run-2 completed 2026-05-19.  A true 5 B
  A/B comparison would take ~10-20 h on each side; deferred until a
  larger Phase 4 batch ships.
- The 5 B-projected wins for #54 (§228) assume the Newton-converged
  Δ between iterations is small (typical case 0-1 adj iters).
- §230's 68-min saving is per validation cycle that re-uses a
  deterministic divisor — it doesn't help fresh first runs (no
  saved r yet) or runs where the divisor changes (e.g., new digit
  count).
